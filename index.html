The solar system is made up of the Sun, eight planets, moons, and other objects in space. -
☀️ Sun: A star that gives us light and heat.
🌍 Planets: Large objects that move around the Sun, like Earth and Mars.
⭐ Stars: Huge balls of hot gas that give off light.
🌙 Moons: Objects that move around planets.
- 🚀 Space objects: Asteroids, comets, and other objects found in space.
